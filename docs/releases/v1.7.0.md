# Faded Balancer DCTL v1.7.0

v1.7.0 adds **Progressive Two-Point Channel Recovery** for faded scans where one RGB channel has a shifted black point, a reduced range, or both.

1. Pick a neutral dark area to align the damaged channel's black point.
2. Pick a neutral bright area to complete a black-anchored range stretch.
3. Use **Auto** when one channel is clearly damaged, or select Red, Green, or Blue yourself when the shot needs manual direction.

The recovery keeps the selected channel's surviving texture and contrast. It does not copy pixels from another channel or recreate detail that has been clipped or lost.

Other improvements in this release:

- Mixing Shadows, Mixing Highlights, and Donor Luminance now affect both mixing methods consistently.
- Channel replacements are predictable when more than one replacement is enabled.
- Labels clarify the difference between legacy Lift, Gamma, Gain, and the Diagnostic Log Preview.
- Preset Tonal Only lets you use a preset's tonal recipe without its optional mixing assistance.

The Windows installer is unsigned. The macOS package is unsigned and not notarized; follow the [installation guide](../INSTALL.md) for the expected operating-system prompts and the manual DCTL option.
